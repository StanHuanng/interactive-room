# Model provenance

`public/models/sofa.glb`, `desk.glb`, `mac.glb`, `chair.glb`, and `room.glb`
are original models procedurally generated by [`blender/build_assets.py`](../../blender/build_assets.py).
All geometry and simple Principled BSDF materials were created for this project.
No third-party assets, textures, models, or artwork are included.

The computer is an original, unbranded desktop-computer design. No logos or
third-party screen artwork are used.

Models use meters, flat shading, glTF Y-up, applied mesh transforms, and a shared
floor-center origin (the computer's origin is its desk contact plane). The room
origin is the center of the walking surface; the floor slab extends below it.

Regenerate with Blender 4.2.3 LTS:

```sh
blender -b -P blender/build_assets.py
blender -b -P blender/render_previews.py
```

The generated triangle counts, sizes, and Blender-space bounds are recorded in
`public/models/manifest.json`. Previews are original renders of the imported GLBs.
